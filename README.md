## 1. Project Overview

This case study demonstrates an investment banking-style leveraged buyout (LBO) financial model developed to evaluate the hypothetical acquisition of Asterion Business Services.

The model assesses acquisition financing, operating performance, debt repayment capacity, exit valuation and private equity sponsor returns.

The objective is to demonstrate the application of financial modelling techniques in evaluating acquisition opportunities, structuring transactions and supporting investment decisions.

## 2. Financial Modelling Capabilities

- **Acquisition Valuation:** Transaction structuring and entry valuation
- **Sources & Uses:** Acquisition financing and sponsor equity requirements
- **Financial Forecasting:** Operating forecasts and cash flow projections
- **Debt Modelling:** Financing structures, interest expense and repayment schedules
- **Cash Flow Analysis:** Free cash flow and debt servicing capacity
- **Equity Valuation:** Enterprise value to equity value bridge
- **Investment Returns:** Sponsor Internal Rate of Return (IRR) and Multiple on Invested Capital (MOIC)
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Entry and exit valuation multiples and investment return sensitivities
- **Investment Assessment:** Scenario analysis and investment committee evaluation

## 5. Investment Analysis & Key Findings

The illustrative base case generates a **Sponsor IRR of 17.7%** and **MOIC of 2.26x** over a five-year investment horizon.

Against an assumed **20% target IRR**, the investment falls below the required return threshold.

The analysis highlights the importance of:

- **Entry Valuation:** Acquisition pricing and initial equity investment
- **Capital Structure:** Debt financing, leverage and repayment capacity
- **Operational Performance:** Revenue growth, EBITDA margins and cash flow generation
- **Deleveraging:** Debt reduction throughout the investment horizon
- **Exit Valuation:** Exit multiples and terminal enterprise value

Potential value creation opportunities include operational improvements, margin expansion, disciplined capital allocation and acquisition price optimisation.
