## 1. Project Overview

This case study demonstrates an investment banking-style leveraged buyout (LBO) financial model developed to evaluate the hypothetical acquisition of Asterion Business Services.

The model assesses acquisition financing, operating performance, debt repayment capacity, exit valuation and private equity sponsor returns.

## 2. Financial Modelling Capabilities

- Acquisition valuation and transaction structuring
- Sources and uses of funds
- Operating forecasts and cash flow projections
- Debt financing, interest expense and repayment schedules
- Free cash flow and debt servicing capacity
- Enterprise and equity valuation
- Sponsor Internal Rate of Return (IRR) and Multiple on Invested Capital (MOIC)
- Entry and exit multiple sensitivity analysis
- Scenario analysis and investment committee assessment

## 5. Investment Analysis & Key Findings

The illustrative base case generates a sponsor IRR of 17.7% and MOIC of 2.26x over a five-year investment horizon.

Against an assumed 20% target IRR, the investment falls below the required return threshold.

The analysis highlights the importance of entry valuation, financing structure, operational improvements, debt reduction and exit valuation in determining investment attractiveness.
